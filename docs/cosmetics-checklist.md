# Store measuring checklist

Print this. Five products per category, most popular first. Fill in the blank
columns at the shelf. Background and the longer list: `cosmetics-run.md`.

**Measure:** widest point of the bottom 30 mm with the cap on (base). If the
cap is wider than the base, write both. Square tubes: both sides. Height with
cap on. Circle R (round) or S (square) in Shape.

**Bring:** digital calipers, this sheet, a pen, phone for a photo of each
tester next to the calipers with the shelf label in frame.

Store key: **S** Sephora, **U** Ulta, **SU** both.
